⚠️ Tactical Reality Check

Leftover Canva Stills & The "No Unnecessary Splicing" Rule

After locking the voiceover to the 200s visual timeline, you will inevitably have leftover exploratory frames or unplaced graphics in Canva. Do not try to shoehorn them into the video at this stage.

View Full Tactics ↗
🚫 1. The Shoehorn Trap
Forcing leftover graphics onto the timeline breaks the locked 3-words/second cadence and stretches runtime unnecessarily without adding core value.
📁 2. Tag as 'Out of Scope'
Move unused Canva graphics into your 📁 Out of Scope folder or save them as social carousel assets for LinkedIn and Twitter.
🚀 3. Shipping Over Gold-Plating
An 85% finished video delivered before Sunday's cohort beats a 99% video stuck in visual perfectionism. Protect the flow state.
